Overview

Research themes

Questions I return to

Select a theme above to dim unrelated work across the page.

01

Multimodal decision agents for markets

Reinforcement-learning traders that read price dynamics together with fundamentals and text, using transformer and xLSTM backbones and conservative on-policy diffusion for more stable portfolio behavior.

02

Graphs as a language for time

Graph and hypergraph models that treat co-movement, influence, and regime structure as first-class objects rather than afterthought features in financial and sequential forecasting.

03

Networks that know the equations

Physics-informed Kolmogorov–Arnold architectures — including PG-KINN — for forward and inverse PDE problems, where numerical structure and learning capacity are designed to meet.

04

Reading cortical dynamics

End-to-end MEG pipelines for preprocessing, source localization, and decoding, with spectral and time–frequency analysis aimed at visual perception and cognitive state.

05

Protocols under adversarial reality

Ethereum-facing systems work: consensus behavior, Solidity contracts, on-chain transaction structure, and security questions around DeFi and cryptocurrency infrastructure.
